Best AI Receptionist in the US for Dental Practices

The best AI receptionist for a US dental practice is not the loudest chatbot. It is the system that answers reliably, books accurately, respects clinical boundaries, and transfers the right calls to staff.

An AI receptionist is a voice-based system that answers inbound calls, understands caller intent, completes routine front-desk tasks, and escalates sensitive issues to humans. For dental practices, the strongest use cases are new-patient booking, hygiene appointments, rescheduling, hours and directions, after-hours coverage, and overflow support during busy front-desk periods.
