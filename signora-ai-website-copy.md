## Problem

Your practice spent real money on Google Ads, referrals, and local marketing to bring that patient to pick up the phone. When the call goes unanswered, every dollar of that budget disappears.

A patient looking for a dentist will call the next clinic on Google within 60 seconds of hitting voicemail. After hours and weekends, your voicemail is your competitor's best friend.

Your front-desk team spends hours every day on scheduling calls, reminders, and rescheduling requests — tasks that don't require a human but drain the staff you need for in-clinic care.

## The Fix

Signora AI acts as a 24/7 virtual receptionist for dental clinics.

It answers incoming calls, identifies the patient’s intent, helps with booking, rescheduling, cancellations, and common questions, then updates the schedule, sends confirmation messages, or transfers the call to a human when needed.

The core promise is simple: help clinics capture every patient opportunity by ensuring every call is answered professionally, instantly, and consistently.

## Six Things Signora AI Handles the Moment a Call Comes In

- 24/7 inbound call answering for dental practices.
- Appointment booking, including collecting patient information, checking availability, booking visits, confirming details, and sending confirmation messages.
- Appointment rescheduling, including locating existing appointments, presenting alternatives, updating schedules, and sending confirmations.
- Appointment cancellation with appointment verification and record updates.
- General inquiry handling for office hours, clinic location, directions, services offered, new patient information, and basic accepted insurance information.
- Human call transfer for billing questions, clinical concerns, treatment planning, complaints, and emergency situations.
- Natural conversation and SMS Confirmations as part of the initial feature set.

## How It Works

1. **Patient Calls In**: A patient calls your clinic line — during peak hours, after hours, weekends, or holidays. Your team may be busy. Signora AI never is.
2. **AI Understands Intent**: Signora AI greets the caller professionally, identifies what they need — booking, rescheduling, a question, or an emergency.
3. **Action Taken Instantly**: Books new appointments, reschedules existing ones, handles cancellations, or answers common clinic questions — all in real time.
4. **Synced & Confirmed**: Calendar is updated automatically. Patient receives an SMS confirmation. Complex or emergency calls are transferred immediately to your staff.

## FAQ

### What exactly does Signora AI do?
It provides a 24/7 inbound call handling solution for dental clinics that answers calls, books appointments, handles scheduling changes, answers common questions, and transfers complex requests to staff.

### Does it work after hours and on weekends?
Yes, the service is designed to answer patient calls 24/7.

### Can it book and reschedule appointments?
Yes, appointment booking, rescheduling, and cancellation are core parts of the service.

### What patient questions can it handle?
It can handle common questions such as office hours, location, directions, services offered, new patient information, and basic insurance information.

### When does a call get transferred to a human?
Calls are transferred for billing questions, clinical concerns, treatment planning, complaints, and emergency situations.

### Will it give patients medical advice?
No, it does not provide medical advice, treatment recommendations, billing dispute resolution, complex insurance verification, or clinical diagnosis.
