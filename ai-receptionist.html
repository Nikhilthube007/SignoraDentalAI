What Is an AI Receptionist?

An AI receptionist is software that answers calls, understands what callers need, completes routine tasks, and transfers complex requests to a human.

An AI receptionist is a voice or chat system designed to handle front-desk communication. It can greet callers, identify intent, collect information, schedule appointments, answer common questions, send confirmations, and route sensitive issues to staff. In a dental practice, an AI receptionist is most valuable when it captures calls that would otherwise become voicemail.

How an AI receptionist works

  1. The patient calls the practice.
  2. The AI receptionist answers and identifies the reason for the call.
  3. Routine requests are handled through approved workflows.
  4. Complex or sensitive requests are transferred to staff.
  5. The patient receives a confirmation when an appointment is booked or changed.

Common business uses

AI receptionists are used by clinics, home services, law firms, real estate teams, and other appointment-driven businesses. Dental practices benefit because phone demand is frequent, repetitive, and directly connected to booked production.

What it should not do

An AI receptionist should not pretend to be a clinician, make medical decisions, or hide when a human handoff is needed. The best systems are clear about what they can handle and quick to escalate what they cannot.
