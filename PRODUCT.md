# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Small and mid-size US dental clinics with 1 to 10 dentists, busy front-desk staff, existing inbound call volume, missed-call leakage, scheduling workload, and demand they do not want to lose to nearby competitors.

## Product Purpose

Signora AI is a 24/7 AI receptionist for dental clinics. It answers inbound patient calls, understands routine intent, books or changes appointments, handles common questions, sends confirmations, and transfers billing, clinical, complaint, treatment, or emergency calls to the right human. Success means fewer missed calls, fewer voicemail dead ends, more booked patients, and less repetitive phone work for clinic staff.

## Brand Personality

Specific, reassuring, and commercially direct. The brand should feel calm enough for healthcare-adjacent trust, sharp enough for practice owners who care about revenue, and focused enough to avoid sounding like a generic AI automation tool.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI SaaS tropes, vague automation claims, futuristic chatbot spectacle, call-center commodity language, and any visual or copy pattern that implies Signora replaces clinical judgment or human care. Avoid looking like a broad healthcare platform; the niche focus on dental front desks is the strategic edge.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with the cost of a missed call, then show the practical fix.
2. Make the product feel operationally real: calls, schedules, confirmations, and handoffs.
3. Keep clinical boundaries obvious so trust is never borrowed from ambiguity.
4. Support the front desk, do not frame the staff as the problem.
5. Prefer proof, specificity, and patient-flow clarity over abstract AI claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA for contrast, focus states, keyboard navigation, touch targets, and readable typography. Respect reduced motion preferences, keep motion decorative rather than required for comprehension, and avoid color-only communication for status or risk.
